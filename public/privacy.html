Privacy

Blob is a card game for a group of friends. It has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking of any kind, so this page is shorter than most.

The short version

Blob stores the name you type in and enough information to keep you in the game you are playing. It does not use cookies, does not contain any analytics or advertising, and does not send anything to anybody else.

What is kept on your device

A few small settings are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are not readable by anyone else:

  • the name you last played under, so you do not have to retype it
  • which game you are currently in, so closing the tab does not throw you out of it
  • your card and text size, and whether sound is on
  • whether you have dismissed the offer to add Blob to your home screen

These are not cookies and this is not tracking. They are what makes the app work at all, which is why there is no consent banner on this site — none is required for storage that is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. You can clear all of it at any time through your browser's normal "clear site data" setting.

What is kept on the server

While a game is running, the server holds the game itself: who is playing, the display names they chose, and the state of the cards. This lives in memory and is gone when the game ends or the server restarts.

When a game finishes, a short record of it is written down — the display names, who won, and when it was played. Nothing in the app currently shows these; they are kept in case a past-games list comes back one day. There is nothing in such a record beyond a first name and a result, and on the current hosting they do not survive the server restarting.

Ordinary web server logs record IP addresses briefly, as every web server does. They are not used for anything and are not linked to players or games.

What Blob never does

  • No cookies. Not one.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising.
  • No third-party scripts. The page you are reading loads nothing from anywhere else.
  • No email addresses, no passwords, no accounts — there is nothing to sign up for.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or handed to anybody.

Children

Blob is a family card game and children are very likely to play it. That is part of why it collects nothing: there are no accounts to create, no way to message a stranger, and nothing to buy. The only personal detail involved is a display name, and it does not have to be a real one.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the email address below and it will be dealt with.

Who is responsible, and how to reach them

Blob is run by Seb Cawthorne as a personal project, and he is responsible for the little data it holds. If you want something removed, want to know what is held about you, or have a question this page does not answer, email seb.cawthorne@outlook.com.

In practice there is very little to ask about — a display name attached to a finished game — and clearing your browser's site data removes everything held on your device straight away, without needing to ask anybody.
