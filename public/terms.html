Terms of use

Blob is free, made for fun, and run by one person. These terms are short because there is not much to say.

It is free, and it is provided as it is

There is nothing to pay for, nothing to subscribe to and nothing to buy. In return, Blob comes with no guarantees: it may be unavailable, it may have bugs, and it may change or stop entirely without notice.

Games can be lost, and sometimes will be

This is the one thing worth reading. Blob keeps games in the server's memory. If the server restarts, is updated, or goes to sleep after a quiet spell, any game in progress is gone and you will need to start a new one. That is a normal part of how it is hosted rather than a fault. Please do not use Blob for anything that matters.

Play nicely

The only thing you can type into Blob is a display name, and everyone at the table sees it. Do not put anything abusive, offensive or personal in it. Do not try to break the game for other people, and do not try to attack or overload the service.

The games themselves

The card games here — Blob, Silly Head, Sevens, Chase the Ace, Cheat and Go Fish — are traditional games that belong to everybody. They are played the way this household plays them, which is not always the way you will have learned them. Where the house rules differ from the published ones, each game's "How to play" says so.
